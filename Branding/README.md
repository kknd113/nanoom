# Nanoom brand direction

Working recommendation: **Held n**. The lowercase n forms a shelter around one small dot. It ties the name to the product's purpose: a person held in prayer by a small circle. This is an original vector exploration, not a trademark clearance or proof of visual uniqueness.

Open index.html for three directions and light/dark/small-size comparisons. Production-friendly SVG masters:
- nanoom-mark.svg: transparent single-color mark.
- nanoom-icon.svg: opaque 1024-square app-icon concept. Corner masking belongs to iOS.
- nanoom-mark-circle.svg: open-circle alternative.
- nanoom-mark-pair.svg: shared-arch alternative.

Name: Nanoom / 나눔. Lowercase wordmark: nanoom. Korean origin is explained simply as sharing; avoid claiming an exact English pronunciation is obvious. Suggested spoken cue: “nah-noom,” to test with actual Korean and English speakers.

Positioning: a private space for the people who already share your life, even between Sundays.
Primary launch line: “A little closer. Even between Sundays.”
Supporting line: “Life shared. Prayer together.”

Voice: quiet, personal, specific. “What’s on your heart?” / “I prayed for you.” / “An update from Anna.” Avoid pressure, rankings, guilt and unearned promises of spiritual outcomes. Emotional richness should come from real people, their faces and considerate follow-up.

Palette keeps the existing app identity: Ink #24354A, Prayer blue #4C6F9B, Mist #EAF0F8, Paper #FAFBFC. Native system rounded typography remains the app's font. Final wordmark outlines and icon adoption should follow the user's review, legibility checks and a proper name/mark clearance process.

Do not change the app name again. Do not add an ornamental cross, dove, praying hands or a generic heart solely to signal the category. Test the recommended symbol at 24px and as an iPhone icon, including monochrome and dark appearance. The central dot may be removed for very small sizes if testing shows it closes up, but preserve its role in the main mark.

These are review assets. The shipping wordmark and app icon have not been replaced automatically.
